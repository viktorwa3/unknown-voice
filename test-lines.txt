THE UNKNOWN - test lines for ElevenLabs Voice Design
=====================================================
Rule: record the SAME line for BOTH the male and female take (identical text),
slow and flat delivery. Dark vowels (o/u/a) survive the formant work; go light
on s/f sibilance -- the highpass + crush make it hiss.

--- PRIMARY A/B LINE (use this one across all 5 style renders) ---
You saw me before you knew my name.
  ( low sibilance, dark vowels, ~3s slow -- ideal for comparing 01-05 )

--- SHORT / STALKING ---
I have worn your voice. It fits.
Come closer. I remember how you.
We are not what you left in the dark.
Do not look for the mouth that speaks.
Stay. The others did.
You are already inside the sound.

--- LOW-VOWEL HEAVY (stress the sub / rumble + formant shift) ---
Under. Below. All the way down.
I am the hollow you grew around.
Come home to the cold.

--- PLOSIVE HEAVY (stress the pulse / crush breakup) ---
Break. Break and be part of it.
Do not blink. Do not turn back.

--- LONGER (stress reverb tails + preverb pre-swell) ---
You keep turning around. That is good. Keep turning.
We will meet in the middle of it.
There is more of us now than there ever was of you.

--- ONE-WORD (test the pre-swell / onset on something tiny) ---
Closer.
Stay.
Mine.
